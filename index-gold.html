Frequently asked questions

Can I generate a 3D model from a photo in this preview?

With the Studio backend connected, yes: AI builds a real 3D model from your photo. Without it, as in a plain preview, a photo becomes a relief hologram estimated from its brightness, which is not true 3D.

Which model formats work?

GLB and GLTF. For GLTF files that reference separate textures or buffers, use a single self-contained GLB instead.

Is my model uploaded anywhere?

No. Files you choose are read locally in your browser and never leave your device.

How accurate will generated models be?

A single photo shows only one side of an object. The AI estimates the hidden geometry, so unseen areas will be approximate.
